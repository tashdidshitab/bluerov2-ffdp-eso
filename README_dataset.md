# BlueROV2 Heavy synthetic control/RL dataset

Generated by `bluerov2_heavy_dataset.py` (numpy only, seeded, reproducible).

    python bluerov2_heavy_dataset.py --episodes 10000 --duration 30 --out dataset --seed 0

10,000 episodes x 30 s at 10 Hz (300 steps), 10 shards of 1,000 episodes (~35 MB each, ~340 MB total, ~65 s to generate).
`sample_450_episodes.npz` (first 450 episodes of shard 0) is included as a sample; everything regenerates exactly from the seed.

## Simulator
- 6-DOF Fossen model: rigid body + diagonal added mass, linear + quadratic damping, restoring forces (buoyancy/weight, CoB offset), Coriolis, current-relative hydrodynamics. Euler integration at 100 Hz, zero-order-hold actions at 10 Hz.
- 8 thrusters (BlueROV2 Heavy: 4 vectored horizontal at 45 deg, 4 vertical), 6x8 allocation matrix, asymmetric T200-style limits (+51.5 / -40 N), deadband, first-order lag, per-thruster gain, battery voltage scaling.
- Nominal parameters are literature-style placeholders (see `NOMINAL`). **Replace with your own identified values** before claiming results for a specific vehicle.

## Domain randomization (per episode)
Mass +-5%, buoyancy +-1.5 N, added mass +-15%, damping +-20%, CoB offset, thruster gain/lag/voltage, mean current 0-0.35 m/s + OU gusts, white disturbance forces, sensor biases.

## Tasks (`task`) and behavior policies (`policy`)
- task: 0 station keeping, 1 waypoint sequence (3-5 waypoints), 2 trajectory tracking (ellipse / Lissajous / helix-like, tangent or fixed heading).
- policy: 0 expert PD+integral controller with randomized gains, 1 expert + Gaussian action noise, 2 expert + low-amplitude sinusoidal thruster excitation (3-12% of range). The controller only sees noisy sensors.
- Faults (15% of episodes): one thruster goes dead or loses 40-80% effectiveness at a random time (`fault_*` fields). The controller is not told.

## Arrays (N episodes, T = 300)
Shape (N, T+1, ...): `gt_eta`, `gt_nu`, `obs_pos`, `obs_depth`, `obs_euler`, `obs_dvl`, `obs_gyro`, `obs_acc`, `dvl_valid`, `pos_valid`, `ref_pos`, `ref_vel`, `ref_yaw`, `ref_yawrate`, `current`, `thrust`.
Shape (N, T, ...): `action` (normalized thruster commands in [-1,1], applied during [t, t+1)), `reward`.
RL transitions: (s_t, a_t, r_t, s_{t+1}) = index t, t, t, t+1. Reward = -(||e_pos|| + 0.3|e_yaw| + 0.02||a||^2) on ground truth; redefine it from the stored states if you prefer.
Per-episode metadata: `task`, `policy`, `fault_*`, `p_*` (randomized parameters), `ctrl_gain_scale`. Full column descriptions are in `dataset_card.json`.
Frames: world NED (z = depth), body FRD.

## Quality check (10,000 episodes)
Position: median RMSE 0.13 m (95th percentile 0.30 m, max 0.59 m, ground truth after 5 s); no episode above 1 m; no NaNs.
Yaw: per-episode RMS tracking error median 3.6 deg (p95 6.3 deg).
Attitude: median per-episode peak |pitch| 8.8 deg (p95 17.5 deg), peak |roll| 7.8 deg (p95 17.9 deg); 4.3% of episodes exceed 20 deg in roll or pitch (mostly noisy/excitation episodes), so filter them if you need near-level data.
Correction notes: earlier versions used attitude gains that were too weak (about half of the episodes pitched beyond 20 deg), excitation amplitudes that were too large, and yaw gains that were too weak (median yaw RMS 11 deg). All are fixed in the current script; regenerate if you have older files.

## Limitations (state these in the paper)
- Simulation only; no sim-to-real validation. Parameters are nominal, not identified from your vehicle.
- No waves, tether, obstacles, or cameras/sonar; sensors are modeled statistically.
- Because the Heavy is over-actuated and the expert uses little of the thrust range, thruster faults barely degrade the expert (median RMSE rises ~5%). Thrusters rarely saturate. For harder fault-tolerance or RL benchmarks, increase current/speed limits, fault severity, or fault probability in the script.
- Behavior data comes from a hand-tuned controller, so it is suited to imitation learning, offline RL and system identification; online RL should use the simulator directly.
